The Lamborghini Legacy

Founded in 1963 by Ferruccio Lamborghini, Automobili Lamborghini began with a vision to create the perfect grand touring car. What started as a challenge to Ferrari has evolved into a symbol of Italian automotive excellence.

Our first masterpiece, the 350 GT, set the standard for what a Lamborghini should be: powerful, beautiful, and uncompromising. This philosophy continues to drive every vehicle we create today.

From the revolutionary Miura to the iconic Countach, from the Diablo to today's Huracán and Aventador lineage, each Lamborghini represents the pinnacle of automotive engineering and design.

Today, we continue to push boundaries with hybrid technology while maintaining the soul that makes every Lamborghini unmistakable. Our commitment to innovation, performance, and Italian craftsmanship remains unwavering.
